# HerPath demo script (about 100 seconds)

A simple screen recording is fine. Speak slowly, click clearly, and let each
screen breathe for a moment.

## 0:00 - 0:12 - Open on the Discover page

Say: "Hi, I am Ibrahim, and this is HerPath. It helps girls and women find
scholarships, internships and mentors, and get every document ready before
the deadline."

Show the hero, then scroll a little so the first cards are visible.

## 0:12 - 0:32 - Search and filters

Type "mentorship" in the search box. The list narrows.

Say: "There are 18 real programmes here. You can search, or filter by type,
education level, region and field. Every card shows a live countdown to its
deadline, so nothing sneaks past you."

Clear the search. Click the Scholarships chip, then set "I live in" to
Nigeria. Point at the Nigerian scholarships.

## 0:32 - 0:52 - Open a detail view

Click a card, for example the MTN Foundation Scholarship.

Say: "Open any opportunity and you see what you get, who it is for, the
steps to apply, and the documents you will need."

Click Save to my list. Close the modal.

## 0:52 - 1:12 - Find my match

Go to Find my match. Leave the answers as Undergraduate, STEM, Nigeria,
Scholarships. Click Show my matches.

Say: "Four quick questions, and HerPath ranks everything by how well it
fits you, with the reasons spelled out. The MTN scholarship is a 100 percent
match for this student, and you can see exactly why."

Scroll through the top matches.

## 1:12 - 1:32 - My list and checklist

Go to My list. Tick off one or two documents and watch the progress bar move.

Say: "Everything she saves lands here with a document checklist. She ticks
things off as she gets them ready, and it all stays saved on her device,
even offline."

## 1:32 - 1:40 - Close

Say: "HerPath. Your future is calling, and now the doors are easier to find.
Thank you."

End on the Discover page or the footer line: "You belong in every room you
dream of. Keep going."
